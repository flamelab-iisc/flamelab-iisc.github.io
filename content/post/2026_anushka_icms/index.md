Anushka has received the best poster award at the 7th International Conference on Molecular Simulation 2026, Bangalore for her work on assessing chemical reactivity of species under thermochemical non-equilibrium, using reactive molecular dynamics simulations.


<!--more-->
Anushka has won the best poster award in the 7th International Conference on Molecular Simulation held at IISc from 4th October to 8th October, 2026.  The poster was titled "Chemical Reactivity under Thermochemical Non-Equilibrium: A Reactive Molecular Dynamics Study".
The work assesses chemical reactivity of species under thermochemical non-equilibrium using reactive molecular dynamics simulations. Congratulations to Anushka!
