Aswin successfully defended his masters thesis titled "Asynchronous computing and low-precision approaches towards accelerating flow simulations" on August 27th, 2026. The objective of his thesis was to introduce asynchronous computing and low-precision frameworks for reducing data movement costs and accelerating computational fluid dynamics simulations. The thesis defense was attended by Professor Ratnesh Shukla, along with other faculty members and students. The FLAME Lab extends its congratulations to Aswin on his accomplishment and is excited to welcome him back as a research assistant.
