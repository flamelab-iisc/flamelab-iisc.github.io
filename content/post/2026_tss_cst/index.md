The paper titled "An a Posteriori Analysis of Cokurtosis PCA‑Based Dimensionality Reduction Using a Neural ODE Solver" co-authored by Tadikonda Shiva Sai, Hemanth Kolla and Konduri Aditya, has been published in Combustion Science and Technology. This paper presents a dimensionality reduction technique for thermochemical scalars to effectively capture stiff chemical dynamics, demonstrating the potential of CoK-PCA-based manifolds to be implemented in reacting flow solvers.

For more information, please visit:
