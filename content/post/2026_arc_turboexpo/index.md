Aritra presents his work on LES of trapped vortex combustors at ASME Turbo Expo to be held in Milan, Italy in June 2026.

<!--more-->
The Turbomachinery Technical Conference & Exposition is the ASME's annual flagship event. Aritra delivered a presentation titled "Large-eddy simulations of an ammonia-fired trapped vortex combustor" following the manuscript authored by Aritra Roy Choudhury and Konduri Aditya that had been accepted through peer review.
